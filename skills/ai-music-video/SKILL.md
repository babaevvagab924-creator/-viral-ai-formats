# AI Music Video

Visuals synced to a song's structure. AI songs and AI clips together are a fast-growing creator format, and a strong clip makes a track shareable.

## Ask (max 2 questions)
- The song: user's own or AI-generated, genre, BPM, structure (or paste their own lyrics)? — required.
- Narrative or mood visualizer? — default: light narrative with one character.

## Rights rules
- Only for music the user has rights to (own, AI-generated under their plan's terms, or licensed).
- Don't reproduce lyrics of songs the user doesn't own; describe the mood instead.
- Don't make the character a lookalike of a real artist.

## Structure (15–60 s, synced to song sections)
| Section | Visual job |
|---|---|
| Intro (0–3 s) | Strongest image, sets world and palette |
| Verse | Story — character in motion, medium shots |
| Pre-chorus | Tension — tighter shots, light changes |
| Chorus / drop | Payoff — biggest visuals, faster cuts, wide shots |
| Outro | Callback to the intro image |

## Workflow
1. **Treatment** — song map (section → seconds → visual idea), character descriptor, palette. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — one per section beat, same character and palette. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — motion matching the energy of each section.
4. **Edit sheet** — clip order with timestamps and cut-on-beat notes.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, [shot type] of [CHARACTER DESCRIPTOR] [action matching the lyric/mood], in [location], [PALETTE + lighting], [STYLE LINE: e.g. anime cel shading / 35mm film / dreamy pastel 3D], cinematic music video look
```

## Video prompt template
```
[Character] [one motion matching section energy: sways slowly / runs / turns into the wind], [environment motion], camera [move], [energy level: calm/building/explosive], character and palette unchanged
```

## Packaging
- Lyric captions only for the user's own lyrics.
- Caption: song title + "full song in bio".
- Hashtags: #aimusic #musicvideo #[genre] #newmusic #aivideo

## Common mistakes
- Visual energy not matching the music — the drop needs the biggest change.
- Too many locations — 2–3 max for a short clip.
- Cuts ignoring the beat.

## Example — chorus of an original synth-pop track
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, wide shot of Lena, early 20s, long copper hair, white oversized jacket, standing on a rooftop as a pink-violet sunset explodes into neon light beams behind her, dreamy pastel 3D style, cinematic`
**Video:** `Lena spreads her arms as light beams pulse outward on the beat, her jacket ripples in the wind, camera fast crane up, explosive energy, character unchanged`
