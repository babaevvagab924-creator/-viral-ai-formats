# POV: You're the [Object]

The camera IS the object. Familiar things seen from an alien angle trigger instant curiosity and are easy to make into a series.

## Ask (max 1 question)
- Which object/animal and comedic or emotional? — default: everyday object, light comedy.

## Two styles
- **True POV** — camera at the object's height/angle; we see the world it sees (hands reaching in, faces looming).
- **Character POV** — object has a cartoon face; we watch it react. Easier for consistency.

## Structure (20–40 s, 6–9 clips)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Birth/arrival** — made, bought, unpacked, dropped |
| 2–12 s | **Normal life** — 2 clips of its routine |
| 12–25 s | **Danger** — the thing every object of this kind fears |
| 25–35 s | **Outcome** — survive, lost, or reborn |
| 35–40 s | **Punchline/emotional line** |

## Hook formulas
- "POV: you're a [object] and [specific fear] is coming."
- "Life of a [object] in 30 seconds."
- "POV: you're the [object] nobody picks."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — object, style, beat list, punchline. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — consistent lens height (true POV) or locked object design (character POV). Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — POV motion: wobble, tumble, being picked up.
4. **Packaging** — text, caption, hashtags.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template (true POV)
```
Vertical 9:16, extreme low first-person view from the perspective of a [object] lying on [surface], [giant hand / face / world] looming toward camera, [setting], wide-angle 14mm distortion, [lighting], photoreal, shallow depth of field
```

## Video prompt template
```
First-person camera as a [object]: [being lifted / tumbling / sliding], [what the world does], motion blur, handheld wobble, [mood]
```

## Packaging
- On-screen: inner thoughts in quotes ("please not the dishwasher").
- Caption: "What should I be next? 👇"
- Hashtags: #pov #povvideo #lifeofa #aivideo

## Common mistakes
- Camera height changing randomly — lock it.
- No danger beat — POV without stakes is just a product shot.
- Too many human faces in focus; keep humans as giant blurred shapes.

## Example — "POV: you're a sock in the washing machine"
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, first-person view from inside a washing machine drum, looking out through the round glass door at a blurred person, soap bubbles drifting, a lone red sock pressed against the glass, wide-angle, soft blue light, photoreal`
**Video:** `First-person camera as a sock: the drum starts spinning, bubbles swirl, the world outside rotates, the other sock tumbles away out of frame, dizzy motion blur`
On-screen: "Where's my other half?"
