# Horror Narration

Slow dread + a whispering narrator + one final image. Horror has some of the highest completion rates of any faceless format because people must see the ending.

## Ask (max 2 questions)
- Sub-genre: rules-horror, creepypasta, liminal, folklore, found-footage? — default: rules-horror.
- Length? — default: 45–60 s.

## Content limits
- Dread, shadows, wrongness — no gore, no self-harm, no real crimes or real victims.
- Never present a fictional story as a true event involving real people or places' residents.

## Structure (45–60 s, 8–12 clips, ~130 words VO)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Cold open** — the rule or the wrong detail ("Rule 1: never look at the third floor window.") |
| 3–15 s | **Normal** — ordinary place, slightly off |
| 15–35 s | **Escalation** — 3 clips, each one notch creepier; sound drops out |
| 35–50 s | **Breaking point** — the rule is broken |
| 50–60 s | **Final image** — hold 2 s, then hard cut to black |

## Hook formulas
- "If you ever see [thing], don't [action]."
- "Rule #[n]: [rule]."
- "I worked night shift at [place]. There's one room we never cleaned."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — premise, rules, VO script with clip markers, final image. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — consistent location, desaturated palette, figure never fully visible until the end. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — very slow motion, subtle wrongness (a door 2 cm more open).
4. **Packaging** — whisper VO direction, sound design notes, caption.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, [shot type] of [ordinary location] at night, [one wrong detail], dim [light source], deep shadows, desaturated teal-grey grade, film grain, VHS softness, eerie stillness, no gore
```

## Video prompt template
```
Almost static shot, [one subtle change: door creaks open slightly / light flickers once / shape shifts in background], camera very slow push-in, unsettling silence, nothing else moves
```

## Sound notes
Room tone → silence before the scare → one low hit on the final image. Whispered or flat, calm VO.

## Packaging
- Caption: "Would you follow rule #3? 👇" or "Part 2?"
- Hashtags: #horrortok #creepy #scarystories #liminalspaces #aihorror

## Common mistakes
- Showing the monster early — the unseen is scarier.
- Jump-scare every clip — escalation needs quiet.
- Bright, saturated frames; keep it dim and desaturated.

## Example — "Rules for the night shift at an empty mall"
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, wide shot of an empty shopping mall corridor at night, one store's shutter half-raised with darkness inside, dim green emergency lights, glossy floor reflections, desaturated, film grain`
**Video:** `Static shot, the half-raised shutter slowly lifts another few centimeters by itself, emergency light flickers once, camera very slow push-in`
VO: "Rule two. If a shutter is open, don't close it."
