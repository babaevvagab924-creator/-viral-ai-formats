# AI Presenter (Disclosed Host)

An original, recognizable host who explains things to camera. A consistent face builds a channel identity without the creator showing their own.

## Ask (max 2 questions)
- Topic/script or bullet points? — required.
- Host style (friendly expert, deadpan comedian, cartoon mascot)? — default: friendly expert, stylized realism.

## Honesty rules (non-negotiable)
- The host is an **original character**, never a real person or a lookalike.
- Never present the host as a real customer, doctor, or eyewitness. No fake testimonials or reviews.
- Disclose AI: on-screen tag ("AI host") and/or caption, plus platform AI labels.

## Structure (20–45 s, 4–8 clips)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Pattern interrupt** — host says the hook line with a gesture |
| 2–8 s | **Problem / question** |
| 8–35 s | **3 points**, one per clip, with B-roll cutaways |
| 35–45 s | **Takeaway + CTA** |

## Hook formulas
- "Stop doing [common mistake]. Here's why."
- "You've been told [myth]. It's wrong."
- "3 things nobody tells you about [topic]."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — host descriptor + character sheet, script split into ≤8-word lines per clip, B-roll list. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — host frames (same setting) + B-roll frames. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — talking clips with exact lines; B-roll with motion.
4. **Packaging** — captions, AI disclosure tag, hashtags.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template (host)
```
Vertical 9:16, medium close-up of [HOST DESCRIPTOR: original character, age range, hair, signature outfit, one accessory], facing camera with [gesture], in [consistent set: studio desk / cozy room / neon backdrop], soft key light, eye-level, [style: stylized realism / 3D mascot], space at top for caption
```

## Video prompt template (talking)
```
[Host] speaks directly to camera with natural head movement and [gesture], subtle blinking, camera static medium close-up, friendly confident tone, face and outfit unchanged. Sound: "[line, max 8 words]"
```

## Packaging
- Small corner tag: "AI host".
- Word-by-word captions.
- Hashtags: #explained #learnontiktok #[topic] #aihost

## Common mistakes
- Long lines → lip-sync drift; split into short clips with cutaways.
- Host's look changing — reuse the character sheet every time.
- Hiding that it's AI — damages trust and can break platform rules.

## Example — "3 mistakes in AI video prompts"
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, medium close-up of Nova, an original host in her late 20s with a short silver bob, round black glasses and a mustard turtleneck, pointing at camera, in a small studio with a neon "PROMPT LAB" sign, soft key light, stylized realism`
**Video:** `Nova points at camera and tilts her head, natural blinking, camera static, confident tone. Sound: "Stop writing your prompts like this."`
