# Myth & Legend Explained

Gods, monsters and epic visuals told in under a minute. Mythology is evergreen, global, and every culture has hundreds of stories — endless series.

## Ask (max 2 questions)
- Which myth/creature? — default: a lesser-known story from a well-known mythology.
- Tone: epic or eerie? — default: epic.

## Respect rules
- Retell faithfully; flag where versions differ ("in some versions…").
- For living religions and Indigenous sacred stories, avoid mockery and sexualization; present them as the culture tells them.
- Don't invent "ancient facts".

## Structure (45–75 s, 9–12 clips, ~150 words VO)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook image** — the monster or the punishment |
| 3–12 s | **Who** — the hero/god and what they want |
| 12–40 s | **Conflict** — 3 clips of escalation |
| 40–60 s | **Climax + consequence** |
| 60–70 s | **Legacy** — what the myth explains or the word it gave us |

## Hook formulas
- "The Greek gods punished him by [punishment]. Here's why."
- "This creature from [culture] folklore [terrifying trait]."
- "The word '[word]' comes from this myth."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — story arc, character descriptors, VO script, legacy fact. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — consistent art direction (painterly epic, fresco-inspired, ink wash) matched to the culture. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — cinematic motion: wind, light rays, slow epic camera.
4. **Packaging** — captions, title, hashtags.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, [shot type] of [CHARACTER DESCRIPTOR with culturally accurate clothing/attributes], [mythic action], in [mythic location], [divine/eerie lighting], [ART DIRECTION matched to culture: painterly epic / ink wash / carved relief style], cinematic composition
```

## Video prompt template
```
[Character] [one mythic action], [elemental motion: wind in cloak, lightning, mist], camera [slow low-angle push-in / epic crane up], awe, character design unchanged
```

## Packaging
- Caption: "Which myth next? 👇"
- Hashtags: #mythology #greekmythology #folklore #legends #aivideo

## Common mistakes
- Mixing art styles from different cultures.
- Making gods look like generic superheroes — use the culture's iconography.
- Missing the legacy beat — it's what people share.

## Example — "Sisyphus"
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, low-angle shot of a muscular bearded man in a torn chiton pushing an enormous boulder up a steep barren mountain, storm clouds above, shafts of grey light, painterly epic style inspired by classical oil paintings`
**Video:** `The man strains and pushes the boulder one step higher, dust slides down the slope, camera slow low-angle push-in, heavy and endless mood`
VO: "Every day, the same boulder. Forever."
