# ASMR / Oddly Satisfying

Impossible materials, perfect cuts, crisp sounds. One of the most-watched AI formats: zero language, endless replays.

## Ask (max 1 question)
- Material + action? — default: knife slicing objects made of an impossible material (glass, crystal, jelly-metal).

## Satisfying ingredients
- **Impossible material** — glass strawberry, crystal bread, lava chocolate
- **Clean action** — slice, press, peel, pour, stack, fit
- **Precise reveal** — the inside is surprising (galaxy inside the glass apple)
- **Sound** — crisp, isolated, close (Veo can generate it; otherwise add ASMR SFX)

## Structure (10–30 s, 3–8 clips)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–1 s | **Blade/hand already touching the object** — no wait |
| 1–20 s | **3–6 actions**, each on a new object or same object deeper |
| last 2 s | **Final reveal** or loop to the first frame |

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — material, objects, action per clip, inner reveal. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — macro, clean surface, consistent backdrop and light. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — slow, precise, single action; describe the sound.
4. **Packaging** — caption, hashtags.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, extreme macro shot of a [object] made entirely of [impossible material] on a [clean wooden board / black marble], a sharp knife blade touching its surface, [soft studio light with reflections], hyperreal, crisp detail, shallow depth of field
```

## Video prompt template
```
Knife slowly slices through the [material object] in one smooth stroke, [what happens: clean glassy halves separate / shards sparkle / inner galaxy revealed], camera static macro, calm satisfying pace. Sound: crisp [glass crunch / crystal ring] ASMR, no music
```

## Packaging
- No text, or just "🔊 sound on".
- Caption: "What should I cut next? 👇"
- Hashtags: #asmr #satisfying #oddlysatisfying #glassfruit #aivideo

## Common mistakes
- Hands distorting — keep hands mostly out of frame; show the blade.
- Fast chaotic cuts — satisfying needs slow and clean.
- Background clutter; one clean surface.

## Example — "Glass kiwi"
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, extreme macro shot of a kiwi made entirely of translucent green glass with tiny black glass seeds on dark walnut wood, a sharp chef's knife touching its top, soft studio light with glints, hyperreal`
**Video:** `Knife slowly slices the glass kiwi in one stroke, two halves fall apart revealing a glowing green core, a few shards sparkle, camera static macro. Sound: crisp glass crunch ASMR, no music`
