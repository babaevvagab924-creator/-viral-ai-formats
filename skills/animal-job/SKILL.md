# Animals With Human Jobs

A serious animal in a serious job. The deadpan contrast is the joke, and animals are the most shared category on every platform.

## Ask (max 1 question)
- Animal + job? — default: pick a pairing where the animal's trait fits the job (owl night guard, sloth DMV clerk).

## Comedy rule
Play it straight. The animal is a competent professional; the humor comes from animal instincts leaking through (cat knocks the cup off the counter after making it).

## Structure (15–35 s, 5–8 clips)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Establish** — animal in uniform, at work, serious |
| 2–15 s | **Doing the job well** — 2 clips of competence |
| 15–25 s | **Instinct breaks through** — the animal moment |
| 25–35 s | **Recover with dignity** — straightens tie, continues |

## Hook formulas
- "Your barista today is a cat."
- "Day 1 of the new security guard."
- "Customer service, but it's a [animal]."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — animal descriptor with uniform, job, instinct gag. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — same animal markings every frame. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — natural animal motion + human-job action.
4. **Packaging** — caption, hashtags, sound.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, [shot type] of [ANIMAL DESCRIPTOR: breed, fur color/markings, eye color] wearing [tiny uniform with name tag], [job action] at [workplace], realistic proportions, [workplace lighting], photoreal, shallow depth of field, comedic deadpan tone
```

## Video prompt template
```
[Animal] [one job action with paws/beak/hooves], [natural animal micro-motion: ear twitch, tail flick], camera [static/handheld], deadpan workplace mood, fur markings and uniform unchanged
```

## Packaging
- On-screen: workplace-style text ("Employee of the month: Biscuit").
- Caption: "What job should she do next? 👇"
- Hashtags: #animals #funnyanimals #catsoftiktok #dogsofinstagram #aivideo

## Common mistakes
- Animals walking upright like humans in photoreal → uncanny. Keep natural anatomy.
- Markings changing between clips — lock them in the descriptor.
- Humor through text only; the instinct moment must be visual.

## Example — "Cat barista"
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, medium shot of a fluffy grey British Shorthair with copper eyes wearing a tiny green barista apron and name tag "Mochi", pressing an espresso tamper with both paws at a café counter, warm morning café light, photoreal`
**Video:** `Mochi places a finished latte on the counter, stares at it, then slowly pushes it toward the edge with one paw, ear twitches, camera static, deadpan`
