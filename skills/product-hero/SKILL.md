# Product Hero Shot (Premium Ad)

High-end commercial look for any product in 10–20 seconds. Small brands look like big brands — and it's the most requested format by paying clients.

## Ask (max 2 questions)
- Product + 2–3 hero ingredients/features? — required.
- Product photo? — strongly recommended.

## Brand rules
- Product shape, label and colors must match the real product (photo reference every frame).
- Show only true features; no fake certifications or claims.

## Shot menu (pick 5–7)
- **Macro texture** — surface, droplets, grain
- **Levitation** — product floating, slow rotation
- **Splash / pour** — liquid crown around the product
- **Ingredient explosion** — ingredients burst outward in slow motion
- **Hero orbit** — 180° around the product on a pedestal
- **Light sweep** — a light bar glides across the label
- **Packshot** — clean centered final with space for logo/CTA

## Structure (10–20 s)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Most striking shot** (splash or explosion) |
| 2–12 s | **3–4 detail/feature shots**, each 2–3 s |
| 12–17 s | **Hero orbit** |
| 17–20 s | **Packshot + CTA** |

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — shot list, color palette pulled from the packaging, mood. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — product photo as reference, same backdrop palette. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — slow-motion physics, one movement per clip.
4. **Packaging** — CTA, music mood (bass hits on cuts), caption.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, [shot from menu] of [PRODUCT exactly as reference: shape, label, colors], [ingredients/liquid], on [backdrop in brand palette], studio lighting with soft key and strong rim light, high-end commercial product photography, ultra sharp, label readable
```

## Video prompt template
```
High-speed slow motion, [liquid splashes around / ingredients burst outward / product rotates slowly in mid-air], camera [slow orbit / static macro], glossy premium mood, product shape and label stay exactly the same
```

## Packaging
- Music: minimal beat, cut on the hits.
- End card: logo + CTA; mark as ad where required.
- Hashtags: #productvideo #commercial #cgi #aiad #[category]

## Common mistakes
- Label morphing during motion — keep motion around the product, not on the label.
- Too many effects in one clip — one per shot.
- Background palette clashing with packaging.

## Example — 1 kg salt pack
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, levitation shot of a 1 kg salt pack exactly as in reference, salt crystals bursting outward around it, on a deep navy gradient backdrop, soft key and white rim light, high-end commercial product photography, label readable`
**Video:** `High-speed slow motion, salt crystals scatter outward and sparkle as the pack slowly rotates in mid-air, camera static, premium mood, pack shape and label unchanged`
