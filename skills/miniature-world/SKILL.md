# Miniature World

A whole tiny civilization in a familiar object. Cozy, detailed, rewatchable — people pause to find little details.

## Ask (max 1 question)
- Big object + what the tiny people do? — default: tiny workers assembling or cleaning a big everyday object.

## Look options
- **Tilt-shift photo** — real places that look like toys (blurred top and bottom).
- **Diorama** — handmade miniature set, felt/wood/clay textures.
- **Tiny people in a giant world** — HO-scale figurines working on oversized food/products.

## Structure (15–30 s, 5–8 clips)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Reveal scale** — wide shot of the giant object with tiny crew |
| 2–15 s | **Work details** — 3 macro clips of tiny tasks (cranes, ropes, ladders) |
| 15–25 s | **Problem or finish** — something slips, or the job completes |
| 25–30 s | **Pull back** — human hand/face enters for scale |

## Hook formulas
- "How burgers are really made."
- "There's a whole city inside your [object]."
- "Tiny workers, giant [thing]."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — object, tiny crew roles, the task story, look option. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — macro lens, tilt-shift blur, consistent figurine style. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — small, busy motions; slow camera.
4. **Packaging** — caption, hashtags, cozy sound.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, macro tilt-shift photograph of a miniature scene: tiny HO-scale figurines of [workers/people] [task] on/inside a giant [object], tiny scaffolding, ladders and cranes, strong tilt-shift blur top and bottom, [warm/soft] lighting, diorama realism, extremely detailed
```

## Video prompt template
```
Tiny figurines [busy task: pull ropes / carry a sesame seed / push a cart], small crane rotates, slightly stop-motion-like movement, camera slow macro slide, cozy busy mood, scale unchanged
```

## Packaging
- Ambient sound: tiny construction noises, cozy music.
- Caption: "Find the worker taking a nap 👀"
- Hashtags: #miniature #tiltshift #tinyworld #diorama #aivideo

## Common mistakes
- Scale inconsistent between clips — always mention "HO-scale figurines" and a giant object.
- No depth-of-field blur → loses the miniature illusion.
- Brand products: label must stay accurate (use photo reference).

## Example — "Tiny crew building a burger"
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, macro tilt-shift photograph of tiny HO-scale construction workers in orange vests lowering a giant sesame bun onto a burger with a miniature crane, ladders against the lettuce, strong tilt-shift blur, warm kitchen light, diorama realism`
**Video:** `The tiny crane slowly lowers the bun as workers pull guide ropes, one worker waves, slightly stop-motion-like movement, camera slow macro slide`
