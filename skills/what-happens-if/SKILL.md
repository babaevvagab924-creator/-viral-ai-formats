# What Happens If…? (Science Hypothetical)

A chain of consequences with a ticking timeline. Curiosity + escalation + "learn something" = high saves and shares.

## Ask (max 1 question)
- The hypothetical? — default: suggest 3 and let the user choose.

## Accuracy rules
- Base consequences on established science; when scientists disagree or it's speculative, say "scientists think" or "probably".
- No fake numbers. If unsure of a figure, use relative terms ("within hours").
- Avoid fear-mongering about real-world risks.

## Structure (40–60 s, 8–10 clips, ~140 words VO)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Event happens** — show it instantly |
| 2–10 s | **Seconds after** |
| 10–25 s | **Hours / days after** |
| 25–40 s | **Years after** |
| 40–55 s | **The unexpected consequence** — the shareable fact |
| 55–60 s | **Question to audience** |

## Hook formulas
- "What happens if [event]? Second 1:…"
- "If [event], you'd have [time] to [action]."
- "This is what [event] would actually do to Earth."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — hypothetical, timestamped consequence chain, VO script, the surprise fact. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — one per timestamp, consistent visual world (same city or same planet view). Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — physical motion: waves, debris, light shifts.
4. **Packaging** — timeline overlay, captions, hashtags.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, [shot type: wide / orbital / street-level] of [scene at this stage of the event], [2 physical consequences visible], [lighting from the event], photoreal cinematic sci-fi realism, scientifically plausible, space at top for timestamp
```

## Video prompt template
```
[Physical process: ocean tide recedes rapidly / sky darkens / debris rises], [secondary motion], camera [slow aerial drift / static wide], epic but grounded, environment consistent with start frame
```

## Packaging
- Timestamp overlay: "+10 seconds", "+1 day", "+100 years".
- Caption: "What hypothetical should I do next? 👇"
- Hashtags: #whatif #science #space #physics #aivideo

## Common mistakes
- Hollywood physics presented as fact.
- Jumping randomly in time — keep a forward timeline.
- Wall of narration with static images — every VO line needs a new visual.

## Example — "What if the Moon disappeared?"
**Frame (+6 hours):** `Vertical 9:16, wide shot of a coastal town at night with no moon in the sky, the harbor water oddly calm and low, boats resting at angles, star-filled sky, photoreal cinematic realism`
**Video:** `Gentle waves barely move in the harbor, stars twinkle, camera slow aerial drift over the bay, calm and eerie`
VO: "Tides would shrink to a fraction of what they are."
