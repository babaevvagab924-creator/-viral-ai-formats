# What If You Ended Up In… (POV World Drop)

The viewer is dropped into another world and sees it through a recurring hero. Works because "you" makes it personal and every episode is a new world — infinite series.

## Ask (max 2 questions, otherwise assume)
- Which world? (era, fantasy, invented) — default: pick a surprising one.
- Recurring hero or pure first-person? — default: recurring hero, 18–25, consistent look.

## Structure (30–45 s, 8–10 clips)
| Time | Beat | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Drop** — hero wakes/falls into the world, wide reveal | Hook: "What if you woke up in ___?" |
| 2–6 s | **First shock** — one detail that's wildly different | Curiosity |
| 6–14 s | **Rules of the world** — 2 clips, each one rule | Learning |
| 14–24 s | **Trouble** — the hero breaks a rule | Tension |
| 24–34 s | **Escape or adapt** — how they survive | Payoff |
| 34–40 s | **Cliffhanger** — portal opens to the next world | Series loop |

## Hook formulas
- "What if you woke up in [world] — and [one rule]?"
- "You have 24 hours to survive [world]."
- "POV: you just landed in [world] and nobody speaks your language."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — world, hero descriptor, hook line, beat list. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — one per beat, same hero descriptor pasted verbatim. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — one per approved frame, one action each.
4. **Packaging** — on-screen text per clip, caption, hashtags, sound idea.
Use `references/models.md` to adapt prompts to the user's tools.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, [shot type] of [HERO DESCRIPTOR], [action], in [world location with 2 specific details], [era-accurate lighting], [style line], cinematic, shallow depth of field, empty space in upper third for text
```

## Video prompt template
```
[Hero] [one action], [secondary motion in environment], camera [move], [mood], face and outfit stay identical to the start frame
```

## Packaging
- On-screen text: one short line per clip, max 7 words, second person ("You can't touch the river.").
- Caption: "Which world should you land in next? 👇"
- Hashtags: #whatif #pov #alternateworld #aivideo #[world]

## Common mistakes
- Explaining the world with narration instead of showing one rule per clip.
- Hero's outfit changing between clips — lock it, or justify the change on screen.
- Ending without a next-world tease — kills series retention.

## Example — "What if you woke up in a city where gravity flips at noon?"
**Frame 1:** `Vertical 9:16, wide shot of Tim, 18, slim, messy dark hair, grey hoodie, white sneakers, waking on a cobblestone street where people walk on the undersides of balconies, warm morning light, stylized realism, empty upper third`
**Video 1:** `Tim sits up and stares upward, a woman strolls past upside down on a balcony above, camera slow tilt up from Tim to the balcony, wonder, Tim's face and hoodie unchanged`
On-screen: "You wake up. Something's wrong."
