# Product Comes Alive (Ad)

The product is the hero and the joke. Feels like entertainment, not an ad, so people watch to the end — and the logo is on screen the whole time.

## Ask (max 2 questions)
- Product + one key benefit? — required.
- Product photo available? — strongly recommended for accuracy.

## Brand rules
- Only for brands the user owns or is hired by. Don't use other companies' logos or mascots.
- The product must stay accurate: shape, colors, label. Use the real product photo as reference.
- No false claims; the benefit shown must be true.

## Structure (15–30 s, 5–8 clips)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Wake-up** — product blinks, twitches or jumps off the shelf |
| 2–10 s | **Personality shows the benefit** (fast → it sprints; fresh → it blooms) |
| 10–20 s | **Mini adventure** — 2 clips |
| 20–27 s | **Lands in the customer's hand** |
| 27–30 s | **Packshot + CTA** |

## Hook formulas
- Visual: the product moving where it shouldn't (crawling out of the bag).
- Text: "Our [product] had enough of the shelf."

## Workflow
1. **Concept** — benefit → personality → adventure, CTA. Wait for approval.
2. **Frame prompts** — product photo as reference in every frame, label facing camera in key frames. Wait for approval.
3. **Video prompts** — playful motion, product shape locked.
4. **Packaging** — CTA text, caption, hashtags, music mood.
See `references/models.md`.

## Frame prompt template
```
Vertical 9:16, [shot type] of [PRODUCT exactly as in reference image: shape, colors, label], [alive action: hopping/peeking/stretching], in [setting tied to the benefit], [lighting], commercial product photography, crisp detail, label clearly readable
```

## Video prompt template
```
[Product] [one playful action], subtle squash and bounce, [environment reacts], camera [tracking/orbit], fun upbeat mood, product shape, colors and label stay exactly the same
```

## Packaging
- End card: product, logo, one-line CTA ("Order now — link in bio").
- Caption: benefit + CTA.
- Mark as ad/sponsored where required.

## Common mistakes
- Product warping or label melting — always use the photo reference and say "label unchanged".
- Adding cartoon faces that cover the label.
- Forgetting the packshot.

## Example — sushi delivery box
**Frame:** `Vertical 9:16, medium shot of a black sushi delivery box with a red logo sticker, lid slightly lifted like a mouth, two salmon nigiri peeking out, on a kitchen counter, warm evening light, commercial food photography`
**Video:** `The box hops twice toward camera and the lid pops open revealing neat rolls, camera slow push-in, upbeat, box shape and logo unchanged`
